## Current Capability-Claims Reconciliation — 2026-08-22

The `0.1.0-alpha` entries above are preserved as historical records of
component presence and development status at that time. They are not evidence
that the full AIOS product, every listed component, or all production behavior
was then complete or is currently production-ready.

Accepted Stage 5–9 verification later established the bounded Registry, Event
Engine, AIOS Core, ingestion, lifecycle, failure-preservation, and operational
foundation claims. Component and integration evidence remains distinct from
production verification: Stage 8 lifecycle evidence was primarily bounded
test/integration verification, while Stage 9 accepted the systemd-managed
production process, single Telegram poller, reboot activation, runtime/source
separation, and security/exclusion boundaries.

The authoritative systemd service artifact and production verification were
accepted later under Stage 9; the historical `systemd Integration` entry does
not backdate that evidence to the alpha record.

Brain, Intelligence/LLM, Memory, Specialist routing, autonomous workflows, and
business-management capabilities remain later-stage or unverified unless
separately implemented and accepted.
