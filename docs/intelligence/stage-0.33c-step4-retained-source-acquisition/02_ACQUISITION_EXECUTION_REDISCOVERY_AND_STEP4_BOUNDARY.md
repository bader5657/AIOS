# Stage 0.33C-P4R Acquisition Execution, Rediscovery, and Step 4 Boundary

## Separately executed acquisition sequence

Only after this governance is independently reviewed and merged unchanged may
the Project Owner provide one genuine eligible source. The ordinary execution
must follow this order:

1. confirm reviewed main and the unchanged current Telegram/Universal
   Ingestion path are deployed and normally authorized;
2. confirm no exceptional filesystem, manifest, Registry, or ingestion bypass
   is required;
3. have the Project Owner submit exactly one genuine preferred source through
   the existing Telegram intake;
4. allow the existing one-message Universal Ingestion and retention lifecycle
   to complete without retries, substitutions, or manual correction;
5. record only bounded execution identities and dispositions outside Git;
6. perform the read-only success checks from document 01 for the one new
   manifest and original; and
7. stop without invoking the harness, querying material-receipt duplicate
   state, constructing an approval package, or creating a candidate.

If the normal service, supported file classification, storage, manifest
validation, or ordinary Registry handoff fails, acquisition fails closed. Do
not resend a changed source, edit files, fabricate a manifest, repair a partial
result, or use a manual filesystem write under this authority. Any exceptional
operation requires separate execution governance.

## Rediscovery requirement

Successful retention is not Step 4 selection or approval. Stage
0.33C-P4S1 read-only discovery must be repeated against the entire current
retained set. The new source must independently pass integrity, relevance,
privacy, 1–10 item eligibility, allowed-unit and quantity compatibility, and
fact-completeness assessment. It may be labelled only with the discovery
statuses allowed by merged Step 4 governance; no package is approved here.

Production material-receipt duplicate preflight is not performed during
retention. It remains pending until separately authorized Step 4 selection and
approval, and must later be refreshed under Step 5/7 governance. Ordinary
Registry activity inherent in existing intake is not a material-receipt
duplicate query and grants no candidate authority.

## Boundaries and success disposition

This governance makes no runtime or production mutation. Future success means
one genuine original plus one current valid manifest retained through the
ordinary path, exact hashes and identities verified read-only, and zero
material-receipt candidates or production candidate writes. It does not require
all trusted facts to be resolved during acquisition.

The future execution evidence must state: source count one; normal mechanism;
recognized media type; manifest identity/hash/validation; original
size/hash/non-symlink verification; verifier retrievability; Registry handoff
disposition without credentials or row contents; legacy records untouched;
PostgreSQL duplicate preflight not performed; harness invocation zero;
authorization artifacts zero; candidates zero; and Step 5 not authorized.

The governed runtime input paths remain absent and must not be created:

- `/opt/aios/runtime/intelligence/production-candidate-create/stage-0.33c/approved-input.json`;
- `/opt/aios/runtime/intelligence/production-candidate-create/stage-0.33c/approved-input-approval.json`; and
- the separate `authorization.json` path.

After successful retention and rediscovery, a separate Project Owner fact review
and exact-package approval task remains necessary. Step 5 becomes eligible only
after full Step 4 selection and closure; it is not authorized by source
retention.
