# Project Owner Approval and Activation

The Project Owner approves exactly one corrected cold rerun under the frozen
contract in this package. The clarification makes the schema's existing
confidence scale explicit; it does not authorize a schema, model, runtime,
sampling-setting, output-token, concurrency, or resource-ceiling change.

Approval is activated only after this package is reviewed and merged and the
operator passes the immediate read-only safety preflight. Activation authorizes
one corrected cold request and its monitoring only.

It does not authorize the twenty warm requests, repeated cold attempts,
production inference, provider integration, Brain wiring, deployment, service
changes, cleanup, restart, or remediation.

After the single rerun, return its unmodified evidence for governance review.
If it is invalid, no further inference is authorized. If it is valid, warm-run
authority still requires a separate execution decision after review of the
rerun and its post-checks.
