# Publication and Activation

## Publication Conditions

- the PR contains only this Stage 5.1.1 governance package;
- the exact baseline and Project Owner approval remain recorded;
- review and mergeability are clean;
- no runtime, test, schema, configuration, database, dependency, architecture,
  Blueprint, Frozen Roadmap, Stage 3, or Stage 4 change enters the PR; and
- normal repository merge policy is used without bypass.

Upon normal merge to `main`, the approved package becomes **Published** as the
official Stage 5.1.1 repository record.

## Activation

The initiating Project Owner instruction explicitly directs approval,
publication, activation, and closure when the resulting contract remains
within the five approved categories and exclusions. After publication and a
passing post-merge closed-world audit, this package becomes **Active** for the
Stage 5.1.1 data-responsibility scope only.

Activation creates no implementation permission and starts no later Stage 5
step.
