# Component and Later-Stage Claim Boundaries

## AIOS Core

Allowed wording must preserve all of these facts:

- stateless;
- deterministic;
- accepts `EventEnvelope` input;
- produces bounded routing-readiness output;
- sole positive target is `AIOS_BRAIN_BOUNDARY`; and
- performs no Brain invocation.

AIOS Core must not be described as an active reasoning Brain, intelligence
runtime, Specialist Router, business router, or completed-response generator.

## Universal Ingestion

Universal Ingestion may be described within its verified Telegram delegation,
RequestContext, storage/metadata/Manifest handoff, Registry/Event/Core
lifecycle, ordering, and failure-preservation scope.

It must not imply arbitrary semantic understanding, complete media-group
orchestration, web retrieval, generalized intelligence, or business completion.

## Mission Control

If referenced, Mission Control must be limited to the accepted formatter role:
static status/version presentation, environment display, bounded image and
Manifest inventory counts, timestamp, and current multiline output. Its static
`Running` label is not service health, liveness, readiness, uptime, alerting,
or remote-control evidence.

## Later-stage exclusions

The following remain `ROADMAP_ONLY` or unverified and must be separated from
the current verified foundation:

- Brain execution;
- Intelligence or LLM reasoning/runtime, including Ollama;
- Memory runtime, embeddings, retrieval, or vector database;
- Specialist Router and Business Specialists;
- Hermes/OpenClaw operational runtime;
- n8n operational workflows;
- broker or queue systems;
- broader autonomous automation; and
- completed semantic AI responses.

Customer domain evidence does not prove current operational management of
customers, orders, products, transactions, HPP, inventory, reports, or other
business workflows. Those claims remain later-stage goals unless separately
accepted operational evidence exists.
