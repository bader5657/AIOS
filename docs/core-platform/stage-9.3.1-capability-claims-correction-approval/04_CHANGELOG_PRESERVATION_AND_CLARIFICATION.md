# CHANGELOG Preservation and Current Clarification Policy

## Historical preservation

The `0.1.0-alpha` record must remain present. Its historical component wording
must not be silently erased or retrospectively rewritten as if later Stage 5–9
evidence existed at that time.

## Authorized clarification model

The later implementation must append one concise section that is dated or
clearly marked as the current capability-claims reconciliation. It must state:

- early entries represented component-presence or development status at that
  time;
- later accepted Stage 5–9 verification narrowed the authoritative meaning of
  current capability claims;
- authoritative systemd artifact acceptance and production verification
  occurred later under Stage 9; and
- historical wording is not proof of current full production capability.

The clarification must be factual, traceable to accepted verification, and
must not announce a new release, capability, completion, or version.

## Historical systemd wording

The existing historical `systemd Integration` line may remain only with the
new clarification that:

`authoritative systemd artifact and production verification were accepted later under Stage 9`

Stage 9 capability must not be falsely dated back to the alpha entry.
