# Brain and Later-Stage Separation

Route success means eligible for downstream handoff to `AIOS_BRAIN_BOUNDARY`.
It does not mean Brain was imported, instantiated, invoked, or responded.

No Brain API/runtime, LLM/model/provider/prompt, Memory, Specialist Router,
Specialist, tool, planner, knowledge, or business-domain dependency or behavior
is authorized. AIOS Core Route remains distinct from Specialist Router.
