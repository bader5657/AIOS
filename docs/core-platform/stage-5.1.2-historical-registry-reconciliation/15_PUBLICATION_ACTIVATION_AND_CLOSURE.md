# Publication, Activation, and Closure

## Publication Conditions

- PR diff contains only this governance package;
- review and mergeability are clean;
- exact current/historical SHAs and Project Owner decision remain recorded;
- no runtime, test, schema, database, PostgreSQL, dependency, architecture,
  Stage 3, Stage 4, or Stage 5.1.1 change enters; and
- normal repository merge policy is used without bypass.

Upon normal merge to `main`, the package becomes **Published** as the official
Stage 5.1.2 disposition record.

## Activation and Closure

The initiating Project Owner instruction explicitly authorizes activation and
closure when the package remains within the approved reconciliation scope.
After publication and passing post-merge audit, the disposition is **Active**
and Stage 5.1.2 is **Closed**.

Final effect:

- Stage 1.2.2 evidence reviewed;
- component-level REJECT reaffirmed;
- no disposition change or restoration;
- conceptual evidence retained only as non-authoritative evidence;
- Registry Entry unresolved;
- Stage 5.1.1 unchanged; and
- no runtime or PostgreSQL authority granted.
