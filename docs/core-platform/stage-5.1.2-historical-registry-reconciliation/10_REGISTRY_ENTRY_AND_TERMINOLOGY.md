# Registry Entry and Terminology Consistency

## Registry Entry

`Registry Entry = UNRESOLVED`

Stage 5.1.2 requires only historical component disposition. It does not require
or authorize fields, dataclass, database row, payload, persistence model,
domain entity, or other concrete record representation. Historical
`RegistryRecord` does not fill this gap.

If later work proves a concrete representation necessary, separate Project
Owner authority must precede its creation.

## Terminology

- `PostgreSQL Registry` remains the canonical Stage 5 capability name.
- `Registry` remains permitted only as shorthand in unambiguous Stage 5
  context.
- No repository-wide equivalence is asserted.
- The Blueprint's Brain-consumable `Registry` term is not interpreted here.

Stage 5.1.1 terminology is unchanged.
