# Reboot, Polling, Monitoring, and Safety Evidence

## Installation, enablement, and pre-reboot gate

The approved unit was installed at `/etc/systemd/system/aios.service`.
`daemon-reload` completed and the effective service matched the approved
artifact. The initial service start, exactly-one-poller check, journald
visibility, PostgreSQL health, Storage read/write check, and clean stop/start
all passed. The unit was enabled.

The pre-reboot gate passed only after confirming:

- one systemd-owned production poller and no predecessor/alternate poller;
- the approved source, interpreter, environment, and loopback DSN boundaries;
- healthy PostgreSQL with preserved data;
- successful clean service lifecycle and monitoring visibility;
- available predecessor rollback evidence; and
- absence of Telegram conflict and restart-loop indicators.

## Controlled reboot and post-reboot state

One controlled reboot was completed. No reboot is performed by this closure.
After reboot, the verified state was:

- Docker: `active`
- PostgreSQL container `aios-postgres`: `healthy`
- PostgreSQL endpoint: `127.0.0.1:5432`, with no public listener
- `aios.service`: `enabled`, `ActiveState=active`, `SubState=running`
- automatic reboot activation: `PASS`
- `MainPID=1615`
- `NRestarts=0`
- Telegram polling process count: exactly `1`
- active command:
  `/opt/aios/runtime/venv/bin/python -m core.adapters.telegram.main`

The post-reboot PID is historical evidence for the verified observation, not a
claim that a PID is a permanent identifier.

## Observability and process safety

- `systemctl status`, `systemctl is-active`, and `systemctl is-enabled` were
  proven usable for the service.
- `journalctl` exposed service lifecycle output and the reboot startup event.
- No Telegram conflict was observed.
- No duplicate polling process was observed.
- No predecessor or alternate polling process was observed.
- No restart loop was observed; `NRestarts=0` at post-reboot verification.

These facts prove systemd as the sole production process owner and satisfy the
Stage 9 monitoring and single-polling policy.
