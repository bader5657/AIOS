# Brain Boundary Audit

AIOS Brain is the named downstream boundary after AIOS Core. Active authority
names Brain components and information Brain may consume, but defines no
runtime Brain API, service contract, invocation permission, or executable
behavior.

Route ends at the Brain boundary. Stage 7.1.1 does not authorize crossing it,
calling Brain, or implementing Intelligence, Memory, Planner, Specialist
Router, Specialists, or business behavior.
